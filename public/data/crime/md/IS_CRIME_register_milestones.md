- **2022-12** [B] Homicide clearance falls to 52.3% — the lowest ever reported — Down from 93% in 1962. Nearly half of US homicides that year were not closed by arrest or exceptional means.

- **2022-06** [A] First national intimidation estimate: ~800,000 offences — BJS's NIBRS National Estimates begin — the first coverage-adjusted national count of the offence that contains stalking. No comparable figure exists for any earlier year.

- **2024-12** [B] Homicide clearance recovers to 61.4% — Back to the 2019 level after the 2020-2022 trough. Roughly four in ten homicides still go uncleared.

- **2026-01** [B] ICE detention passes 70,000 held in a single day — Reported two ways for the same month: about 73,000 on 16 January (CBS, citing ICE) and 70,766 on 24 January (Kocher, from ICE's published detention files). Vera counts 456 facilities in active use against 220 ICE publicly acknowledges; ~444,900 bookings since January 2025.
