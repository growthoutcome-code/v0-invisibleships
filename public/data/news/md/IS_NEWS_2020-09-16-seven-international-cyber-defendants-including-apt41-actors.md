# Seven International Cyber Defendants, Including "Apt41" Actors, Charged In Connection With Computer Intrusion Campaigns

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2020-09-16. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/seven-international-cyber-defendants-including-apt41-actors-charged-connection-computer>*

The Justice Department announced on 16 September 2020 that five Chinese nationals and two Malaysian nationals have been charged over computer intrusion campaigns that it says hit more than 100 companies and organisations in the United States and abroad. Victims named in the release include software and hardware makers, telecom providers, social media and video game companies, universities, think tanks, foreign governments and pro-democracy activists in Hong Kong, and the alleged conduct includes theft of source code and data, ransomware and cryptocurrency mining.

The department says the two Malaysians were arrested in Malaysia on 14 September 2020, the five Chinese nationals remain fugitives, and hundreds of accounts, servers and domains were disabled. These are charges only: every defendant is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
