# Hospital Researcher Sentenced to Prison for Conspiring to Steal Trade Secrets, Sell Them in China

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2021-02-01. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/hospital-researcher-sentenced-prison-conspiring-steal-trade-secrets-sell-them-china>*

A former researcher at Nationwide Children's Hospital's Research Institute in Ohio was sentenced to 30 months in prison for conspiring to steal exosome trade secrets and conspiring to commit wire fraud, the Justice Department announced on 1 February 2021. She was also ordered to pay $2.6 million in restitution and forfeit about $1.25 million and shares in two companies.

According to the department, she set up a company in China to sell exosome isolation kits. Her husband, also a former researcher there, had pleaded guilty and was awaiting sentencing.
