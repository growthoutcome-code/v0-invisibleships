# Amazon Bedrock Opens at AWS Top Secret Cloud

*ExecutiveBiz, 2025-01-01. Original: <https://www.executivebiz.com/articles/amazon-bedrock-platform-top-secret-cloud-availability>*

Amazon Web Services announced on 14 January 2025 that Amazon Bedrock, its generative AI service, is generally available to U.S. government customers in its Top Secret classified cloud, ExecutiveBiz reports. The models offered include Anthropic's Claude 3.5 Sonnet and Amazon Titan Text Embeddings V2, for use by national security organisations.
