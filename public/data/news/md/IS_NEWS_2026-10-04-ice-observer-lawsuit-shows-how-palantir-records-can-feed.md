# ICE observer lawsuit shows how Palantir records can feed border screening, analytics

*Biometric Update, 2026-10-04. Original: <https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ice-observer-lawsuit-shows-how-palantir-records-can-feed-border-screening-analytics>*

Biometric Update reported on 4 October 2026 on a class action in the U.S. District Court for the District of Maine brought by people who observed immigration arrests in January 2026. The plaintiffs allege that a federal agent photographed observers and entered records on eight of them into Palantir's Investigative Case Management system as possible threats to law enforcement, in retaliation for activity protected by the First Amendment; they are suing the Department of Homeland Security, Customs and Border Protection and Immigration and Customs Enforcement.

According to the report, a government filing dated 18 September 2026 acknowledges that one such record led to a plaintiff's extra border screening in March 2026, and DHS says its policy forbids targeting people solely for exercising First Amendment rights. The publication says the plaintiffs want the records archived and DHS barred from using them, and that the case is ongoing.
