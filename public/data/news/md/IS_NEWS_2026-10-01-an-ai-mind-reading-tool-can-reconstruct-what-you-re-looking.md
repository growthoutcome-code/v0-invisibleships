# An AI “mind-reading” tool can reconstruct what you’re looking at from a brain scan

*MIT Technology Review, 2026-10-01. Original: <https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/10/01/1145588/ai-mind-reading-reconstructs-what-youre-looking-at/>*

MIT Technology Review reported on 1 October 2026 that researchers at the Weizmann Institute of Science in Israel have built an AI system that reconstructs images a person is looking at from functional MRI brain scans, and can also predict brain activity from images. According to the report, the work was presented at the Cognitive Computational Neuroscience conference in New York, and the system needs about one hour of scan data from a new person, compared with around 40 hours for earlier methods.

The publication says the system still makes mistakes and so far works only with still images. It reports that scientists warn such tools could raise mental-privacy concerns, especially if adapted to less invasive EEG recordings.
