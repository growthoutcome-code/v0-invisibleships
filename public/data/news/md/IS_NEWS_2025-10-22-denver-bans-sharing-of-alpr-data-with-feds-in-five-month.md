# Denver bans sharing of ALPR data with feds in five-month Flock extension

*Denver7, 2025-01-01. Original: <https://www.denver7.com/news/front-range/denver/denver-bans-sharing-of-license-plate-reader-data-with-the-feds-as-part-of-five-month-flock-contract-extension>*

Denver's mayor announced a five-month, no-cost extension of the city's contract with Flock Group for license plate reader cameras that bars sharing the data with federal agencies, Denver7 reported on 22 October 2025. Under the agreement Flock faces a $100,000 fine for each instance of sharing data with the federal government, and outside law enforcement agencies must sign agreements not to share the data federally.

Denver7 reports the extension was made without a city council vote; the council had unanimously rejected a contract expansion in May over privacy concerns, and some council members criticized the deal. Any longer-term contract would need council approval.
