# Egypt launches gov cloud computing data center

*DatacenterDynamics, 2024-04-29. Original: <https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/egypt-launches-govt-cloud-computing-data-center>*

Egypt's president inaugurated the country's first government data and cloud computing center, on the Ain Sokhna highway, on 28 April 2024, DatacenterDynamics reports. The site covers more than 23,500 square meters, with 10,000 in use, and is meant to centralize ministry data, act as a disaster recovery site and support AI use across government.

DatacenterDynamics says more than 15 local and international companies helped build it, and that the government previously stored much of its data abroad.
