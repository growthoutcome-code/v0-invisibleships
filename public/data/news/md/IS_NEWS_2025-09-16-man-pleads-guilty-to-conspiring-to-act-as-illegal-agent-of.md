# Man Pleads Guilty to Conspiring to Act as Illegal Agent of the Chinese Government in the United States

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2025-09-16. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/man-pleads-guilty-conspiring-act-illegal-agent-chinese-government-united-states>*

A 68-year-old resident of Queens, New York, a naturalized U.S. citizen and former Chinese dissident who led a pro-democracy nonprofit, pleaded guilty to conspiring to act as an agent of China's government without notifying the Attorney General, the Justice Department announced on 16 September 2025. According to the department, from 2018 to June 2023 he gathered information for China's Ministry of State Security on U.S.-based democracy activists, including joining their encrypted chat groups, and was paid and met officers in China.

The charge carries up to five years in prison. Sentencing in federal court in Manhattan is set for 29 January 2026.
