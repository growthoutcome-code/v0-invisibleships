# Five individuals charged variously with stalking, harassing and spying on U.S. residents on behalf of the PRC

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2022-03-01. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/five-individuals-charged-variously-stalking-harassing-and-spying-us-residents-behalf-prc-0>*

Five people have been charged in cases over alleged efforts to stalk, harass and spy on U.S. residents on behalf of China, the Justice Department announced on 16 March 2022. The department says the targets included Chinese dissidents and pro-democracy activists, and one scheme sought to undermine a congressional candidate.

Charges vary by defendant and include acting or conspiring to act as an agent of China, interstate harassment, misusing identification and conspiring to bribe a federal official. Three defendants in New York were arrested on 15 and 16 March 2022 and two in China remain at large. These are charges only: every defendant is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
