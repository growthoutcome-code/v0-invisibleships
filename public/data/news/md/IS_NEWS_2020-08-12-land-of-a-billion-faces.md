# Land of a billion faces

*MIT Technology Review, 2020-08-12. Original: <https://www.technologyreview.com/2020/08/12/1006665/clearview-ai-billion-faces>*

MIT Technology Review reports that Clearview AI has built a database of about one billion facial images, taken from public photos on sites including LinkedIn, Facebook, Twitter, YouTube and Venmo. Its facial recognition search is used by law enforcement agencies, including the FBI and ICE, to identify people in investigations.

Several platforms, including Twitter, have sent the company cease-and-desist letters demanding it stop collecting their users' images and delete them.
