# AWS pledges to spend $5B in Mexico

*TechCrunch, 2025-01-14. Original: <https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/14/aws-pledges-to-spend-5b-in-mexico-launches-new-mexico-server-region>*

Amazon Web Services announced on 14 January 2025 that it will invest $5 billion in Mexico over 15 years and launched its AWS Mexico (Central) server region in Querétaro, TechCrunch reports. AWS estimates the investment will add $10 billion to Mexico's GDP and support about 7,000 full-time-equivalent jobs, mostly at suppliers rather than at AWS itself.

TechCrunch notes that data center job estimates often exceed what is delivered, and that Querétaro is in a drought; AWS says the facility will be air-cooled and will not use water for cooling during operations.
