# Tech CEO, Russian National Arrested on Complaint Alleging They Hid Russian Ownership and Development of Software Sold to U.S. Government

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2026-09-23. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/tech-ceo-russian-national-arrested-complaint-alleging-they-hid-russian-ownership-and>*

The U.S. Attorney's Office for the Central District of California announced on 23 September 2026 that two people were arrested on a complaint charging conspiracy to commit wire fraud. Prosecutors allege they hid that Oxygen Forensics, a Virginia company selling digital forensics software, was owned and controlled by Russian nationals and that its software was developed in Russia, while the company won contracts with federal agencies including the Department of War, the Secret Service and Homeland Security Investigations.

According to the release, one defendant was arrested in Idaho and the other at London Heathrow Airport, with extradition expected. The office says the case was investigated by the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security and the Defense Criminal Investigative Service. These are charges only: every defendant is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
