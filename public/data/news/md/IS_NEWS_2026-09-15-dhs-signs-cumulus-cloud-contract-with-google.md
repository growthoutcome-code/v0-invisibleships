# DHS signs Cumulus cloud contract with Google

*Washington Technology, 2026-09-15. Original: <https://www.washingtontechnology.com/contracts/2026/09/dhs-signs-cumulus-cloud-contract-google/415994/>*

Washington Technology reported on 15 September 2026 that the Department of Homeland Security has signed a Cumulus cloud contract with Google's public sector unit, worth up to $875.6 million over five years (a one-year base period and four option years). The contract covers infrastructure, platform and software services for use across the department.

The publication says this is the third of four planned Cumulus awards, after Amazon Web Services in June 2026 and Oracle in August 2026, with Microsoft's still being completed. DHS then plans a separate competition for Cumulus operations support.
