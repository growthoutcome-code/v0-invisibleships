# Active-Duty and Former U.S. Army Soldiers Arrested for Theft of Government Property and Bribery Scheme

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2025-03-06. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/active-duty-and-former-us-army-soldiers-arrested-theft-government-property-and-bribery>*

Two active-duty U.S. Army soldiers stationed at Joint Base Lewis-McChord and one former soldier were arrested and charged following grand jury indictments, the Justice Department announced on 6 March 2025. Two face bribery and theft of government property charges in Oregon; the third is also charged in Washington state with conspiring to obtain and transmit national defense information.

According to the department, from November 2021 to December 2024 they gathered military information, including on Bradley and Stryker vehicles and HIMARS, and tried to sell classified hard drives to people in China, receiving at least $15,000. These are charges only: every defendant is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
