# Denmark bans Chromebooks and Google Workspace in schools over data transfer risks

*TechCrunch, 2022-07-18. Original: <https://techcrunch.com/2022/07/18/denmark-bans-chromebooks-and-google-workspace-in-schools-over-gdpr>*

Denmark's data protection agency, Datatilsynet, has barred schools in Helsingør municipality from using Google Chromebooks and Google Workspace, TechCrunch reports. The regulator found that Google's data processing terms allow student data to be transferred outside the EU for support purposes without adequate protection under the GDPR.

The ban took effect immediately and the municipality was told to delete user data by 3 August 2022. TechCrunch reports the agency expects other Danish municipalities to take similar steps.
