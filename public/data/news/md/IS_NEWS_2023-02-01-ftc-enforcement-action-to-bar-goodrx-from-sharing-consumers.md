# FTC enforcement action to bar GoodRx from sharing consumers' health information

*Federal Trade Commission, 2023-02-01. Original: <https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/02/ftc-enforcement-action-bar-goodrx-sharing-consumers-sensitive-health-info-advertising>*

The Federal Trade Commission announced in February 2023 an enforcement action against GoodRx, which it says shared users' sensitive health information with Facebook and Google for advertising and failed to report those disclosures. The action would bar GoodRx from sharing health information for advertising and includes a $1.5 million civil penalty, according to reports.

It was the first enforcement action under the FTC's Health Breach Notification Rule, according to legal commentary on the case.
