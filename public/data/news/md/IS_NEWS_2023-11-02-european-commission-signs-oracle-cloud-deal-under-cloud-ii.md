# European Commission signs Oracle cloud deal under Cloud II

*The Register, 2023-11-02. Original: <https://www.theregister.com/2023/11/02/european_commission_oci>*

The European Commission signed a six-year, €15.75 million deal with Oracle to provide Oracle Cloud Infrastructure and platform services to EU institutions, The Register reports. The contract followed a competitive procurement and gives access to more than 100 cloud services.

According to The Register, the Commission already buys cloud services from AWS and OVHcloud under its multi-cloud approach; the Cloud II framework has a total capacity of €417.7 million.
