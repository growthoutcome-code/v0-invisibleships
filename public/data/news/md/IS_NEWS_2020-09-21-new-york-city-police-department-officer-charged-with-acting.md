# New York City Police Department Officer Charged with Acting As an Illegal Agent of the People's Republic of China

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2020-09-21. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/new-york-city-police-department-officer-charged-acting-illegal-agent-people-s-republic-china>*

A New York City Police Department officer, who was also a U.S. Army reservist with a secret-level security clearance, was arrested on 21 September 2020 and charged with acting as an illegal agent of the People's Republic of China, wire fraud, making false statements and obstructing an official proceeding, the Justice Department says. Prosecutors allege that since at least 2014, at the direction of officials at China's New York consulate, he reported on Chinese citizens in the New York area, identified possible intelligence sources in the Tibetan community and helped Chinese officials gain access to senior police leaders.

The false statement charge relates to a security clearance form on which he allegedly denied foreign contacts. These are charges only: every defendant is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
