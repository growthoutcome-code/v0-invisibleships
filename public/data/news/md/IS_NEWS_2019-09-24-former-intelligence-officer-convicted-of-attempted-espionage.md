# Former Intelligence Officer Convicted of Attempted Espionage Sentenced to 10 Years in Federal Prison

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2019-09-24. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/former-intelligence-officer-convicted-attempted-espionage-sentenced-10-years-federal-prison>*

A former Defense Intelligence Agency civilian case officer, who is also a retired U.S. Army warrant officer, was sentenced on 24 September 2019 to 10 years in federal prison for attempted espionage, the Justice Department says. He had pleaded guilty in March 2019 to attempting to pass national defense information to China.

According to the department, between 2014 and 2018 he met regularly with Chinese intelligence agents, solicited classified information from a DIA colleague, and obtained documents on U.S. military readiness that he intended to hand over. The department says he received hundreds of thousands of dollars for information he provided.
