# Healthcare campaigners turn up the heat on Palantir at Labour conference

*The Register, 2026-09-29. Original: <https://www.theregister.com/public-sector/2026/09/29/healthcare-campaigners-turn-up-the-heat-on-palantir-at-labour-conference/5299724>*

The Register reported on 29 September 2026 that health campaign groups held protests at the Labour Party conference in Liverpool, urging the UK government not to extend Palantir's role in the NHS Federated Data Platform. The report says doctors' and health workers' unions also oppose an extension.

According to The Register, the contract could be worth up to £330 million over seven years, its first term ends in early 2027, and 30 September was the contractual deadline for Palantir to provide an exit plan. It notes that the House of Commons Science, Innovation and Technology Committee has recommended the contract not be extended, and that Palantir says the NHS owns the data the platform processes.
