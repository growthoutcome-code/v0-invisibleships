# DTA chief defends AWS pick for COVIDSafe

*iTnews. Original: <https://www.itnews.com.au/news/dta-chief-defends-aws-pick-for-covidsafe-547844>*

The head of Australia's Digital Transformation Agency defended the choice of Amazon Web Services to host the COVIDSafe contact-tracing app at a Senate inquiry into the COVID-19 response, iTnews reported on 6 May 2020. The agency said AWS offered development and operations services beyond plain hosting and could scale quickly, and that the deal followed an earlier Department of Home Affairs arrangement under a whole-of-government agreement.

According to iTnews, the contract, signed on 24 April 2020, is worth $710,000 to October, within a total project cost of $1.5 million.
