# WHO Director-General's opening remarks: COVID-19 characterized as a pandemic

*World Health Organization, 2020-03-11. Original: <https://www.who.int/news-room/speeches/item/who-director-general-s-opening-remarks-at-the-media-briefing-on-covid-19---11-march-2020>*

The Director-General of the World Health Organization said at a media briefing on 11 March 2020 that the agency had assessed that COVID-19 could be characterized as a pandemic. According to the WHO, there were more than 118,000 cases in 114 countries and 4,291 deaths at that point, and cases outside China had risen 13-fold in two weeks.

The Director-General said the label did not change the WHO's assessment of the threat or its advice, and that countries could still change the course of the outbreak through detecting, testing, treating, isolating and tracing contacts.
