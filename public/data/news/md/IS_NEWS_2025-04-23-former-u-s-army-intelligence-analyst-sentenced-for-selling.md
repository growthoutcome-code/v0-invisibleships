# Former U.S. Army Intelligence Analyst Sentenced for Selling Sensitive Military Information

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2025-04-23. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/former-us-army-intelligence-analyst-sentenced-selling-sensitive-military-information>*

A former U.S. Army intelligence analyst who held a Top Secret clearance was sentenced to 84 months in prison for selling sensitive military information to a person in China, the Justice Department announced on 23 April 2025. He had pleaded guilty in August 2024 to conspiring to collect and transmit national defense information, unlawfully exporting controlled information, and bribery.

According to the department, from May 2022 to March 2024 he provided about 92 documents, including technical manuals for military aircraft and missiles, for about $42,000. The case was heard in the Middle District of Tennessee.
