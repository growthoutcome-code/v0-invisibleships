# Six paths to the nonsurgical future of brain-machine interfaces

*Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA), 2019-05-20. Original: <https://www.darpa.mil/news/2019/nonsurgical-brain-machine-interfaces>*

DARPA announced on 20 May 2019 that six teams would receive funding under its Next-Generation Nonsurgical Neurotechnology (N3) program, which seeks brain-machine interfaces that can both record from and send signals to the brain without surgery, for able-bodied service members. According to the agency, four teams are working on systems entirely outside the body.

DARPA says the other two teams are building "minutely invasive" systems that include nanotransducers delivered to the brain without surgery; one of them describes electromagnetic nanotransducers that convert neurons' electrical signals into magnetic signals and back. The release describes a four-year program with human trials planned for its final phase.
