# Microsoft quietly launches Israeli Azure cloud region

*DatacenterDynamics, 2023-10-31. Original: <https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/microsoft-quietly-launches-israeli-azure-cloud-region>*

Microsoft has opened its Israel Central Azure cloud region near Modiin, between Tel Aviv and Jerusalem, DatacenterDynamics reports. The region was first announced in 2020 and had been expected in 2021, then early 2022; it was initially listed with three availability zones, later marked as coming soon.

According to DatacenterDynamics, Microsoft was the last of the major U.S. cloud providers to open an Israeli region, after Oracle, Google and AWS, and did not respond to questions about the launch. AWS and Google won Israel's $1.2 billion Project Nimbus government cloud tender in 2021.
