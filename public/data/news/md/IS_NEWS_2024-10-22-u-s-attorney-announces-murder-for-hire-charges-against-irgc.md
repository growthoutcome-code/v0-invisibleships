# U.S. Attorney Announces Murder-For-Hire Charges Against IRGC Brigadier General And Former Intelligence Officer And Members Of An Iranian Intelligence Network

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2024-10-22. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/us-attorney-announces-murder-hire-charges-against-irgc-brigadier-general-and-former>*

The U.S. Attorney's Office for the Southern District of New York announced on 22 October 2024 murder-for-hire and related charges against seven men, including a brigadier general in Iran's Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, a former Iranian intelligence officer and members of an Eastern European criminal group. Prosecutors allege that from July 2022 the Iran-based network hired the group to kill a U.S. citizen journalist and human rights activist in New York who criticizes the Iranian government, and that one member was arrested near the victim's home that month with an assault rifle.

Two defendants are in U.S. custody, one was extradited to Georgia, and the four Iran-based defendants remain at large. These are charges only: every defendant is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
