# Arrests for alleged treasonous espionage, gathering of state secrets, attempted treason and spying

*Der Generalbundesanwalt beim Bundesgerichtshof, 2026-10-06. Original, in German: <https://www.generalbundesanwalt.de/SharedDocs/Pressemitteilungen/DE/2026/Pressemitteilung-vom-06-10-2026.html>. Title translated from the German.*

Germany's Federal Public Prosecutor General announced on 6 October 2026 that two German nationals were arrested on warrants from an investigating judge of the Federal Court of Justice: a former president of a German intelligence agency and a long-serving office manager. The prosecutor says it suspects the former official of treasonous espionage, gathering state secrets, attempted treason and intelligence-agent activity. It suspects the second person of aiding him.

According to the release, about 2,000 classified documents are alleged to have been passed on over roughly twelve years. The release names an employee of a foreign intelligence service's security apparatus as one intended recipient, but says it is not known whether the material reached that person. It also says the former official is suspected of giving political information over several years to a member of another state's intelligence service. Searches took place in Bavaria, North Rhine-Westphalia and Berlin, and both people were to be brought before the investigating judge. No charges have been filed: both are presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
