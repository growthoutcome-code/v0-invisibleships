# Medical software in Russia (EGISZ / EMIAS)

*TAdviser, 2025-01-01. Original: <https://tadviser.com/index.php/Article:Medical_software_in_Russia>*

TAdviser's reference article on medical software in Russia tracks how the country regulates software as a medical device. It reports that the health regulator Roszdravnadzor set new classification criteria in February 2020, that a government decree in November 2020 allowed one-stage registration for certain software, and that in 2021 the regulator registered its first AI medical software in the highest risk class, a tool for analysing X-rays.

TAdviser also says the regulator simplified software updates in December 2022, and that in 2023 the Ministry of Digital Development set up an industrial competence center for import substitution of pharmaceutical software.
