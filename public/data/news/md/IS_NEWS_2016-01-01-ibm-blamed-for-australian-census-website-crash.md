# IBM blamed for Australian census website crash

*Computer Weekly, 2016-01-01. Original: <https://www.computerweekly.com/news/450403576/IBM-blamed-for-Australian-census-website-crash>*

Two official inquiries placed most of the responsibility on IBM for the failure of the Australian Bureau of Statistics' online census on census night in August 2016, Computer Weekly reports. According to the report, the site withstood a first distributed denial-of-service attack but went down during later ones, and an incorrectly installed router kept IBM from restoring it quickly.

Computer Weekly reports that IBM was paid $9.6 million to build the site and reportedly paid the government $30 million in a settlement. The inquiries recommended an open tender before the 2021 census and closer scrutiny of procurement; IBM had made no public statement since September 2016.
