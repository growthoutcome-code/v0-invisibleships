# AWS signs deal with US govt for up to $1bn in cloud discounts

*DatacenterDynamics, 2025-08-07. Original: <https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/aws-signs-deal-with-us-govt-to-provide-up-to-1bn-in-cloud-discounts>*

Amazon Web Services agreed with the U.S. General Services Administration on 7 August 2025 to provide up to $1 billion in savings for federal agencies, DatacenterDynamics reports. The credits cover cloud adoption, modernization and training and run through the end of 2028, under the GSA's OneGov program launched in April 2025.

DatacenterDynamics notes that Microsoft, Oracle and Google have made similar discount agreements with the federal government.
