# Israel's military surveillance agency stores masses of intelligence on Palestinians on Microsoft cloud

*DatacenterDynamics, 2025-08-06. Original: <https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/israels-military-surveillance-agency-stores-masses-of-intelligence-on-palestinians-on-microsoft-cloud>*

Israel's military surveillance agency, Unit 8200, stores audio of millions of phone calls made by Palestinians in Gaza and the West Bank on Microsoft's Azure cloud, DatacenterDynamics reports, citing a joint investigation by The Guardian, +972 Magazine and Local Call. According to the report, about 11,500 terabytes of data were held in Microsoft data centres in the Netherlands, with more capacity in Ireland, under an arrangement that went live in 2022.

Sources quoted in the investigation say the data was used in military operations. Microsoft said it was not aware of surveillance of civilians or collection of their calls, and had found no evidence to date that its technology was used to target or harm people in Gaza.
