# California Woman Arrested on Charges Alleging She Acted as Agent of China and Spied on Relatives of U.S. Based High-Ranking Taiwanese Government Official

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2026-10-05. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/california-woman-arrested-charges-alleging-she-acted-agent-china-and-spied-relatives-us>*

The Justice Department announced on 5 October 2026 that a California woman was arrested on one count of acting as an unregistered agent of the People's Republic of China. According to the department, the complaint alleges that she met officials in China in June 2025 to receive instructions. It says that between June and September 2025 she carried out surveillance missions in the Pacific Northwest on family members of a high-ranking Taiwanese government official living in the United States, and passed details and vehicle information to officials in China by messaging app.

The department says the case was investigated by the FBI's Los Angeles Field Office and is being handled by the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Central District of California and the National Security Division. It says the charge carries a maximum of 10 years in prison. This is a charge only: the defendant is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
