# Denver City Council approves ShotSpotter extension ($4.7M, 5yr)

*Denver7, 2022-01-01. Original: <https://www.denver7.com/news/local-news/denver-city-council-approves-long-contract-extension-with-gunshot-detection-company-shotspotter>*

Denver City Council voted 10-1 to extend its contract with gunshot detection company ShotSpotter for five years at $4.7 million, Denver7 reports. The service, used in Denver since 2015, uses audio sensors to locate gunfire.

According to Denver7, a police division chief said 85% of the company's alerts from 2018 to 2021 did not match a 911 call. Critics told the council the system is overpriced and unreliable and that the money would be better spent on mental health and crime prevention.
