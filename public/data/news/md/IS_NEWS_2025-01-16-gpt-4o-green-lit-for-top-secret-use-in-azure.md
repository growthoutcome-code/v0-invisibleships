# GPT-4o green-lit for top secret use in Azure

*DefenseScoop, 2025-01-16. Original: <https://defensescoop.com/2025/01/16/openais-gpt-4o-gets-green-light-for-top-secret-use-in-microsofts-azure-cloud>*

Microsoft has received authorization for 26 more products in its Azure Government Top Secret cloud, including Azure OpenAI Service, which gives access to OpenAI's GPT-4o model, DefenseScoop reported on 16 January 2025. The accreditation is under Intelligence Community Directive 503, which means federal agencies handling top-secret information can now use the service.

DefenseScoop notes that Azure OpenAI received FedRAMP High authorization in August 2024, and that GPT-4 was placed in an air-gapped test environment in May 2024.
