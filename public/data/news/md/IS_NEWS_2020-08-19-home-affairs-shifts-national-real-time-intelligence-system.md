# Home Affairs shifts national real-time intelligence system to Azure cloud

*iTnews, 2020-08-19. Original: <https://www.itnews.com.au/news/home-affairs-shifts-national-real-time-intelligence-system-to-azure-cloud-551904>*

Australia's Department of Home Affairs has moved the National Criminal Intelligence System, which gives federal, state and territory police shared access to criminal intelligence, onto Microsoft's protected-level Azure cloud, iTnews reports. The system is run with the Australian Criminal Intelligence Commission and replaces a database introduced in 1984.

According to iTnews, the move was decided in December 2019 and completed earlier in 2020. The first phase has funding of A$118.2 million, and the project has been delayed partly by COVID-19.
