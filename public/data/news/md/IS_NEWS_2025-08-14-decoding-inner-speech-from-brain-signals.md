# Decoding inner speech from brain signals

*National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke (NIH), 2025-08-14. Original: <https://www.ninds.nih.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/decoding-inner-speech-brain-signals>*

Researchers at Stanford University decoded inner speech, words a person silently imagines saying, from implanted electrodes in the motor cortex of four participants with speech impairment from ALS or stroke, the National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke reports. Error rates ranged from 14% to 33% with a 50-word vocabulary and from 26% to 54% with a 125,000-word vocabulary.

To keep private thoughts from being decoded unintentionally, the team tested safeguards, including a keyword the participant thinks to switch decoding on, which the system recognised more than 98% of the time. The study was published in Cell on 14 August 2025.
