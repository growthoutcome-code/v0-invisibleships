# Navy Neptune Awards Four JWCC Cloud Task Orders

*ExecutiveBiz. Original: <https://www.executivebiz.com/articles/navy-jwcc-aws-azure-google-oracle-task-orders>*

The Navy's Neptune cloud management office awarded consolidated task orders under the Pentagon's Joint Warfighting Cloud Capability contract to Amazon Web Services, Microsoft, Google Public Sector and Oracle on 17 June 2026, ExecutiveBiz reports. Each vendor's cloud services for the Navy and Marine Corps now sit under a single task order.

The Navy says the change is meant to speed up buying and lower costs through volume discounts.
