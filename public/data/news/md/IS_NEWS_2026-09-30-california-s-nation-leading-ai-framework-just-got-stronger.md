# California's nation-leading AI framework just got stronger

*Office of the Governor of California, 2026-09-30. Original: <https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/09/30/californias-nation-leading-ai-framework-just-got-stronger-governor-newsom-signs-more-first-in-the-nation-worker-protections-and-more/>*

The Governor of California's office announced on 30 September 2026 that a further set of artificial intelligence bills had been signed into law. They include limits on workplace surveillance (AB 1331 and AB 1883), rules on automated decision systems in employment (SB 947), notice requirements for job losses caused by technology (SB 951), rules on AI in health care services (AB 1979 and SB 503), and changes to the California AI Transparency Act.

Bloomberg Law reports that AB 1883 bars employers from using AI-powered monitoring tools to collect workers' neural data or to infer their emotional states, with civil penalties of up to $500 per violation. According to the same report, the governor's signing statement asked the legislature to clarify the scope of AB 1883 and AB 1331.
