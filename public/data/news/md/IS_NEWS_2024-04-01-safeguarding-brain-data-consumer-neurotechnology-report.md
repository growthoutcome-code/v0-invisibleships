# Safeguarding brain data: consumer neurotechnology report

*Neurorights Foundation, 2024-04-01. Original: <https://perseus-strategies.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/FINAL_Consumer_Neurotechnology_Report_Neurorights_Foundation_April-1.pdf>*

The Neurorights Foundation published a report in April 2024 reviewing the privacy policies and user agreements of 30 consumer neurotechnology companies. It found that 29 of the 30 appear to have unrestricted access to users' neural data, and that 29 could likely transfer data to third parties in practice.

According to the report, fewer than half let users both withdraw consent and delete their data, and only three combine de-identification, encryption and breach notification. The authors say the analysis covers the documents only and makes no claims about company intent.
