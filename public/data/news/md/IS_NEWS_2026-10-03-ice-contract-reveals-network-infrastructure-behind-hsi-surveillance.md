# ICE contract reveals network infrastructure behind HSI surveillance operations

*Biometric Update, 2026-10-03. Original: <https://www.biometricupdate.com/202610/ice-contract-reveals-network-infrastructure-behind-hsi-surveillance-operations>*

Biometric Update reported on 3 October 2026, from federal procurement records, that Immigration and Customs Enforcement awarded a sole-source contract on 25 September 2026, worth about $703,000 over a base year and two option years, to keep up the dedicated internet circuits used by Homeland Security Investigations for court-authorised (Title III) wiretaps and by a linguistics unit. According to the report, the network links HSI's main site in Lorton, Virginia, and its regional sites to about 50 telecommunications providers over static IP addresses.

The publication says ICE's justification states that an unplanned change of internet provider in April 2025 left wiretaps down for months, and that this was the reason for renewing with the current provider without competition. It also reports other ICE contracts that support wiretap collection, monitoring and transcription.
