# Hospital Researcher Sentenced to Prison for Conspiring to Steal Trade Secrets and Sell to China

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2021-04-20. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/hospital-researcher-sentenced-prison-conspiring-steal-trade-secrets-and-sell-china>*

A former researcher at Nationwide Children's Hospital's Research Institute in Ohio was sentenced to 33 months in prison for conspiring to steal trade secrets on exosomes and conspiring to commit wire fraud, the Justice Department announced on 20 April 2021. He had pleaded guilty in December 2020.

According to the department, he and his wife, also a researcher there, set up a company in China to sell exosome isolation kits. She was sentenced to 30 months in February 2021.
