# Facial recognition: French authority imposes penalty payment on Clearview AI

*European Data Protection Board, 2023-05-01. Original: <https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/national-news/2023/facial-recognition-french-sa-imposes-penalty-payment-clearview-ai-non-payment_en>*

France's data protection authority, CNIL, imposed an overdue penalty payment of €5.2 million on Clearview AI for failing to comply with its earlier order, according to reports on the decision. In October 2022 the CNIL had fined the company €20 million over its use of facial recognition.
