# ATO is rewiring its cloud responsibility model

*iTnews, 2024-01-01. Original: <https://www.itnews.com.au/news/ato-is-rewiring-its-cloud-responsibility-model-611021>*

The Australian Taxation Office is changing how it manages cloud services, moving from a central gatekeeping model to one where responsibility is shared with development teams, iTnews reports. A senior cloud program director told an AWS conference in Canberra that the cloud services team had moved into the office's developer experience branch about three months earlier.

According to iTnews, the office is adding developer self-service and a FinOps program for cost management, which the director said depends on teams such as finance and asset management and on proper tagging of resources.
