# Southern California Political Operative Arrested on Federal Complaint Alleging He Acted as Illegal Agent of People's Republic of China

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2024-12-19. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/usao-cdca/pr/southern-california-political-operative-arrested-federal-complaint-alleging-he-acted>*

A 64-year-old Chino Hills, California, man who managed a 2022 city council campaign was arrested on 19 December 2024 on a federal complaint charging him with acting as an illegal agent of the Chinese government and conspiracy, the Justice Department says. Prosecutors allege he sent reports on a local politician's election to Chinese government officials, helped plan a pro-China friendship association, co-drafted a proposal seeking an $80,000 budget for pro-China activities, and in August 2023 travelled to China with the elected official.

The charges carry maximum sentences of 10 and 5 years in prison. These are charges only: every defendant is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
