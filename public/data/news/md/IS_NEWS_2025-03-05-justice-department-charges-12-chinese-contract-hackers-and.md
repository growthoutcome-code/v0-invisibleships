# Justice Department Charges 12 Chinese Contract Hackers and Law Enforcement Officers in Global Computer Intrusion Campaigns

*U.S. Department of Justice, 2025-03-05. Original: <https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-charges-12-chinese-contract-hackers-and-law-enforcement-officers-global>*

The Justice Department announced on 5 March 2025 charges against 12 Chinese nationals in computer intrusion campaigns: eight employees of the Chinese company i-Soon, two officers of China's Ministry of Public Security, and two members of the hacking group APT27. According to prosecutors, targets included U.S. religious and human rights groups, journalists, federal and state agencies including the Treasury Department, and foreign ministries of Taiwan, India, South Korea and Indonesia, with i-Soon charging $10,000 to $75,000 per hacked email inbox.

The department says all defendants remain at large; the U.S. offered rewards of up to $10 million and seized related domains. These are charges only: every defendant is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.
