# COVID-19 pandemic triggers 25% increase in prevalence of anxiety and depression worldwide

*World Health Organization, 2022-03-02. Original: <https://www.who.int/news/item/02-03-2022-covid-19-pandemic-triggers-25-increase-in-prevalence-of-anxiety-and-depression-worldwide>*

Global prevalence of anxiety and depression rose by 25% in the first year of the COVID-19 pandemic, the World Health Organization said in a scientific brief released on 2 March 2022. The WHO says young people and women were hit hardest, along with people with existing physical health conditions.

According to the WHO, mental health services were severely disrupted and many people still could not get care by the end of 2021. It says 90% of surveyed countries had added mental health support to their COVID-19 response plans, while governments spent on average just over 2% of health budgets on mental health in 2020.
