# New lipid nanoparticle design improves precision of mRNA vaccine delivery

*Penn Engineering, University of Pennsylvania, 2026-03-24. Original: <https://www.engineering.upenn.edu/stories/new-lipid-nanoparticle-design-improves-precision-of-mrna-vaccine-delivery/>*

University of Pennsylvania engineers report a redesigned lipid nanoparticle, the fatty carrier that delivers mRNA in vaccines, published in the Journal of the American Chemical Society. According to the university, in animal studies the new particles sent at least ten times less mRNA to the liver than the formulation used in the Moderna COVID-19 vaccine, while delivery to the lymph nodes, where immune responses are trained, stayed similar.

The university says the particles produced antibody responses comparable to those of clinically approved formulations. The results are from animal models; no human testing is reported.
