# war crimes

*Site-authored. This term was written for invisibleships.com to explain the work; it is not extracted from the source document series, and it carries no source-document id for that reason. Terms drawn from the primary record are in `glossary/` and do carry one.*


Serious violations of the laws of armed conflict, defined in Article 8 of the Rome Statute, such as wilful killing, torture or deliberately attacking civilians. They can only be committed during an armed conflict, international or internal.

Related terms: [crimes against humanity](/glossary/crimes-against-humanity), [atrocities](/glossary/atrocities), [International Criminal Court](/glossary/international-criminal-court), [tribunal](/glossary/tribunal)

Sources: [Definitions — UN Office on Genocide Prevention and the Responsibility to Protect](https://www.un.org/en/genocide-prevention/definition)
