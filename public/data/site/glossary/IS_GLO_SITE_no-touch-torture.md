# No-touch torture

*Site-authored. This term was written for invisibleships.com to explain the work; it is not extracted from the source document series, and it carries no source-document id for that reason. Terms drawn from the primary record are in `glossary/` and do carry one.*


noh·tuhch TOR·cher

Coercion or harm inflicted without physical contact — such as sensory deprivation or overload, sleep deprivation, isolation, and psychological manipulation. A recognized concept in interrogation and human-rights literature. In this work, it is understood to be delivered through Neuro-engagement — the brain-to-brain channel by which sensations and signals can be imposed on a person's nervous system with no physical contact at all.
