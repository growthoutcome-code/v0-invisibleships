# nanomedicine

*Site-authored. This term was written for invisibleships.com to explain the work; it is not extracted from the source document series, and it carries no source-document id for that reason. Terms drawn from the primary record are in `glossary/` and do carry one.*


The use of nanotechnology to prevent, diagnose or treat disease.

Most of the field works on delivery: getting a medicine or genetic instruction to the right cells, in the right amount, without it breaking down on the way. Other areas include imaging agents that make diseased tissue easier to see, and treatments designed to cross the [blood–brain barrier](/glossary/blood-brain-barrier), which blocks most medicines from reaching the brain.

Documented real-world example: the [lipid nanoparticle](/glossary/lipid-nanoparticle) in the mRNA COVID-19 vaccines is the most widely used nanomedicine to date, given in billions of doses.

Related terms: [nanotechnology](/glossary/nanotechnology), [nanoparticle](/glossary/nanoparticle), [lipid nanoparticle](/glossary/lipid-nanoparticle), [blood–brain barrier](/glossary/blood-brain-barrier)
