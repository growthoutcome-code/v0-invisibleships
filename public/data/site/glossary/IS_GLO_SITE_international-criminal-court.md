# International Criminal Court (ICC)

*Site-authored. This term was written for invisibleships.com to explain the work; it is not extracted from the source document series, and it carries no source-document id for that reason. Terms drawn from the primary record are in `glossary/` and do carry one.*


The world's first permanent international criminal court, based in The Hague. It tries individuals, not states, for genocide, war crimes, crimes against humanity and the crime of aggression.

It was created by the Rome Statute, a treaty adopted in Rome on 17 July 1998 that entered into force on 1 July 2002. It is a court of last resort: it complements national courts rather than replacing them, and acts when a country is unwilling or unable to investigate or prosecute.
