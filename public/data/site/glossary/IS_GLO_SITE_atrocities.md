# atrocities (atrocity crimes)

*Site-authored. This term was written for invisibleships.com to explain the work; it is not extracted from the source document series, and it carries no source-document id for that reason. Terms drawn from the primary record are in `glossary/` and do carry one.*


The United Nations' umbrella term for the three most serious international crimes: genocide, crimes against humanity and war crimes. Ethnic cleansing is grouped with them, though it is not a separate crime in law. The UN's 2014 Framework of Analysis for Atrocity Crimes sets out the warning signs for preventing them.

Documented cases: the 1994 genocide in Rwanda, where the 1998 judgment against a Rwandan official was the first genocide conviction by an international court; Srebrenica in July 1995, ruled a genocide on appeal in 2004, with the Bosnian Serb army commander's life sentence upheld in 2021; and Cambodia, where the Khmer Rouge tribunal convicted surviving leaders of genocide in 2018.

Related terms: [crimes against humanity](/glossary/crimes-against-humanity), [war crimes](/glossary/war-crimes), [International Criminal Court](/glossary/international-criminal-court), [tribunal](/glossary/tribunal)
