# Diving

*Site-authored. This term was written for invisibleships.com to explain the work; it is not extracted from the source document series, and it carries no source-document id for that reason. Terms drawn from the primary record are in `glossary/` and do carry one.*


DY·ving

*also called breaching*

A military / telepathic term for establishing a brain-to-brain connection with another human being or animal for the purpose of observation and/or investigation via image-based search.

**Breaching is the earlier name for the same process.** The author used that word before learning the term Diving, and it is the word the record itself uses: *breaching* appears 271 times across 125 journal entries, while *diving* appears in none of them. A reader working through the journal will meet the older word first, and should read the two as one thing.
