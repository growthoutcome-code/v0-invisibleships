- **1999-01** [A] Overdose record begins: 16,849 deaths — The first year of the modern comparable series. Age-adjusted rate 6.1 per 100,000. Every later figure on this track is measured against this. — CDC/NCHS · Data tables for NCHS Data Brief 428: Drug Overdose Deaths in the United States, 1999-2020 <https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/databriefs/db428-tables.pdf>

- **2002-03-09** [A] Lancet publishes first adjusted national suicide rates for China (1995-99) — Phillips, Li & Zhang: 23/100k (~287,000 deaths/yr) after adjusting for unreported deaths; rural 3x urban; baseline for China's later decline to WHO-estimated 8.75 by 2019. — The Lancet · Phillips, Li & Zhang — Suicide rates in China, 1995-99 <https://www.thelancet.com/journals/lancet/article/PIIS0140-6736(02)07954-0/abstract>

- **2006-01-01** [A] Russia implements 2006 alcohol control policy — Later attributed an immediate, permanent ~9% reduction in male suicide mortality (~4,000 male lives/yr) — part of Russia's decline from among the world's highest rates. — AJPH via PMC · Pridemore et al. — 2006 Russian Alcohol Policy and Male Suicide Mortality <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3828708/>

- **2010-01** [A] Second wave of opioid epidemic begins (heroin) — CDC dates the second wave to 2010. Overdose deaths that year: 38,329 — already more than double 1999, before fentanyl enters the record. — CDC · Understanding the Opioid Overdose Epidemic <https://www.cdc.gov/overdose-prevention/about/understanding-the-opioid-overdose-epidemic.html>

- **2013-01** [A] Third wave of opioid epidemic begins (illicit fentanyl) — CDC dates the third wave of opioid overdose deaths to 2013, driven by illicitly manufactured fentanyl (see W4 c09 source). — CDC · Understanding the Opioid Overdose Epidemic <https://www.cdc.gov/overdose-prevention/about/understanding-the-opioid-overdose-epidemic.html>

- **2016-01** [A] Synthetic-opioid acceleration: 63,632 deaths (+21.4%) — The steepest year of the fentanyl wave to that point, three years after it began. Age-adjusted rate 19.8 per 100,000, more than triple 1999. — CDC/NCHS · Data tables for NCHS Data Brief 428: Drug Overdose Deaths in the United States, 1999-2020 <https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/databriefs/db428-tables.pdf>

- **2017-04-07** [B] India passes Mental Healthcare Act 2017, decriminalizing attempted suicide — In force 2018-05-29; presumption of severe stress supersedes IPC 309 — directly relevant to India's police-report undercount. — Wikipedia · Mental Healthcare Act, 2017 <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_Healthcare_Act,_2017>

- **2018-07-26** [A] England & Wales lower coroners' standard of proof for suicide conclusions — Threshold moved to 'balance of probabilities'; intentional self-harm coding rose while undetermined intent fell — a natural experiment in how legal process shapes suicide statistics. — ONS · Change in the standard of proof used by coroners <https://www.ons.gov.uk/peoplepopulationandcommunity/birthsdeathsandmarriages/deaths/articles/changeinthestandardofproofusedbycoronersandtheimpactonsuicidedeathregistrationsdatainenglandandwales/2020-12-08>
