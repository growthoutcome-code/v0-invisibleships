### Global — dq01
[A] documented by WHO — WHO · Suicide data <https://www.who.int/teams/mental-health-and-substance-use/data-research/suicide-data>

Most WHO Member States lack good-quality vital registration for suicide

*Quantification:* 86 of 183 Member States have good-quality vital registration (GHE 2021); the 37 LMICs with good registration account for only 16.3% of estimated LMIC suicides; 73% of global suicides occur in LMICs.

*Document:* WHO suicide data page + fact sheet

### Global — dq02
[A] documented by WHO — WHO · Suicide data <https://www.who.int/teams/mental-health-and-substance-use/data-research/suicide-data>

Systematic misclassification of suicides into other ICD categories

*Quantification:* Most commonly misclassified as deaths of undetermined intent (Y10-Y34), accidents (V01-X59), homicides (X85-Y09), unknown cause; 'under-reporting and misclassification are greater problems for suicide than for most other causes of death.'

*Document:* WHO suicide data page

### Russia — dq03
[A] documented by Semyonova, Sabgayda, Zubko & Gavrilov — European Journal of Public Health · Semyonova et al. — Russian suicide mortality: official and actual rates <https://academic.oup.com/eurpub/article/30/Supplement_5/ckaa166.1371/5915576>

Rising share of Russian deaths coded 'undetermined intent' masks suicides; official decline overstated

*Quantification:* Official suicide mortality fell 3.4x (M) / 2.9x (F) 2000-2018 but undetermined-intent deaths fell only 14.2% / 11.6%; true suicide mortality likely exceeds Rosstat by ~60% (M) and >2x (F); latent events >1/3 (M) and ~50% (F) of total by 2018.

*Document:* European Journal of Public Health 30(Suppl 5), 2020

### Russia — dq04

Age-specific quantification of latent suicides in Russian undetermined-intent coding

*Quantification:* Counting relevant undetermined-intent deaths as latent suicides increases male estimated losses from ~80% (15-24) declining to ~50% at older ages; female suicide mortality rate doubles at all ages above 15.

*Document:* Population and Economics (2024)

### UK (England & Wales) — dq11
