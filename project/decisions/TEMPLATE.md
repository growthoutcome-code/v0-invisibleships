## The question

What was actually being decided, in two or three sentences. Someone reading this in
six months has no memory of the conversation.

## Decision

The conclusion, stated flatly and early. One paragraph.

## Why

The reasoning that actually drove it, including any evidence. Numbers, measurements
and quotes beat assertions.

## Consequences

What this commits us to, including anything it makes harder. Name the trade honestly;
a decision with no cost is usually a decision that was not examined.

## What would change this

The condition under which reopening it is legitimate — a traffic threshold, a law
passing, a product change. Without this, a decision either calcifies or gets
relitigated on a whim.
